Straphanger records the subway rides you take in New York City and shows which stations and lines you have covered.

Unofficial app. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Metropolitan Transportation Authority.

What it does

- Shows a map of the subway network with 496 stations. Color the lines by their MTA colors or by how often you have ridden them.
- Shows statistics for your rides: total rides, miles ridden, and unique miles of track covered.
- Lists every ride with its origin, destination, distance, duration, and whether it ran express or local.
- Lets you log a ride by hand. Pick an origin, an optional transfer, and a destination, or tap a station on the map.

Ways to add rides

- Import a Google Maps Timeline file from your phone. Straphanger finds subway segments, merges transfers made within 15 minutes, and skips duplicates.
- Share a Google Maps transit route to the app.
- Turn on Google Maps notifications. Straphanger reads Google Maps transit navigation notifications to record the ride you are taking. It ignores notifications from other apps.
- Turn on station geofences. Straphanger uses your location, including when the app is closed, to notice when you enter a station and records a ride when you enter a second station at subway speed. This is optional and off until you turn it on. The app explains it on a screen before it asks for permission.

Your data

- Rides are stored on your phone.
- There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.
- Your location, rides and notifications are not sent to the developer or to anyone else.
- You can export all rides to a file and restore them from that file.

Subway data comes from the MTA GTFS feed and the MTA Subway Stations dataset on data.ny.gov. The data is provided as is and may not be accurate, complete or current.
